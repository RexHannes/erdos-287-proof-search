# BANKED — trusted results and audited evidence

This directory is a **semantic index**, not a second copy of the mathematical source tree.

A result belongs here only when it is trusted at a precisely stated scope. Folder membership does **not** mean “Lean-proved”. Evidence classes remain separate:

- [`formal/`](formal/) — Lean/kernel-checked finite or algebraic mathematics;
- [`audited-analytic/`](audited-analytic/) — paper-level analytic results accepted by the current audited bank at their stated hypotheses;
- [`certificates/`](certificates/) — deterministic finite/numerical certificates and replay material;
- [`manuscripts/`](manuscripts/) — audited synthesis manuscripts and their status ledgers.

The physical source and proof-search programme remain subject to the claim firewall in [`../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`](../CLAIMS_LEDGER.md). A formal identity does not automatically prove an infinite Euler product, contour estimate, source instantiation, or final effectivity statement.
