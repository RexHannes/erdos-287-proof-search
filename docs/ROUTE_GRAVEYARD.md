## Rule

`FALSE`, `RETIRED`, and `SUPERSEDED` are not synonyms:

- **FALSE** requires a counterexample, contradiction, or explicit retraction;
- **RETIRED** means the route should not be used as the controlling theorem;
- **SUPERSEDED** means a newer source coordinate has replaced it strategically.

A superseded or retired route may still contain correct and reusable local mathematics. Preserve it in `archive/`; do not promote it back to `frontier/` without first addressing the recorded reason for retirement.
