\subsection{Ford source recovered at paper/research level}
The authoritative source is Ford--Maynard, \emph{On the theory of prime-producing sieves},
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14368}.  Their source architecture includes
\[
G(m;n)=\mathbf 1_{m\le n^\gamma}\,\lambda(m_1)
        g\!\left(\mathbf v(m_2;n)\right),
\qquad
m=m_1m_2,
\]
with threshold factorisation
\[
P^+(m_1)<n^\sigma\le P^-(m_2),
\qquad
H(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}G(d;n).
\]
For $\lambda=\mu$ on the strictly smooth cell $P^+(n)<n^\sigma$, the threshold
factorisation forces $m_1=d$, $m_2=1$.  Since the fixed certificate has
$g_*(\varnothing)=1$, one obtains pointwise
\[
\boxed{
H_*(n)=\sum_{\substack{d\mid n\\ d\le n^\gamma}}\mu(d)
     =\operatorname{truncMobius}\!\left(n,\lfloor n^\gamma\rfloor\right)}.
\]
The strict inequality is load-bearing: the generic non-strict condition
$P^+(n)\le n^\sigma$ is not promoted to the same statement.
